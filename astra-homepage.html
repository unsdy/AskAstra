How it works

Brief Astra once.
She works forever.

No need to keep checking travel sites. You tell Astra what you want — she does the watching and tells you when it matters.

01

Tell Astra

Tell her about a trip — already booked, thinking about booking, or somewhere you'd love to go. A destination, rough dates, a budget. That's all she needs to get started.

02

She watches

Astra checks flights, seats, hotels, cars, nearby airports, and flexible dates around the clock — far more frequently than you'd remember to check yourself.

03

You act

When Astra finds something worth your attention, she texts you — explaining exactly what changed, what it means, and what to do next. Simple. Specific. Human.

The alert experience

A text from someone
who was watching.

Astra doesn't fire off generic notifications. She texts you like a knowledgeable friend who spotted something — with the number, the context, and the reason it matters to you specifically.

01
Specific numbers, always
"$238 below average" beats "prices have dropped." The number is the news — lead with it every time.
02
Your goal, referenced back
"Your target was $520. This is your moment." Proves Astra was listening the whole time.
03
Honest urgency — never manufactured
"These fares typically hold 2–4 hours" — true, verifiable, from real data. No fake countdown timers.
04
Act or defer. Nothing else.
Book now, or snooze an hour. Two options. Every extra choice halves the chance of any action at all.
