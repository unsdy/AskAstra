02 · Astra Brand Identity

A name from the stars.
A voice from earth.

"Astra" (Latin: stars) carries celestial authority while the copy keeps her grounded and human. This tension — cosmic intelligence, plain language — is the brand's personality engine.

Name Meaning

Astra

From the Latin astra — stars. Implies watching, omniscience, guidance. She can see things from above that you can't see from the ground. But she texts you in plain English.

astra → astral → astronomy
always watching · never sleeping
above the noise · working for you
Colour Palette

Celestial & Warm

Deep void backgrounds with violet and aurora accents. Warm amber for alerts. Mint green for savings. Every colour earns its place by carrying meaning.

Typography

Serif + Sans

Cormorant Garamond for display — elegant, editorial, timeless. Geist Sans for UI — clean, confident, precise. Geist Mono for data and labels — technical trust.

Voice Principle

Smart Friend, Not Bot

Astra is brilliant but never shows off. She gives you the number, the reason, and the action — in that order. She never says "I have detected" or "a price change has occurred." She says "It dropped. Here's why it matters."

✗ Never say
"A price change of $89 has been detected on your monitored route."
✦ Always say
"Your London flight just dropped $89. This is the cheapest it's been this month. Want me to hold your eye on it?"

The 5-Point Astra Alert Standard

01
Specific Numbers, Always
"$238 below average" not "prices have dropped." "$489" not "a great price." The number is the news — lead with it every time.
02
Reference the User's Own Goal
"Your target was $520" — this proves Astra was listening and makes the alert personal, not generic. The user remembers why this matters.
03
Honest Urgency — Never Manufactured
"These fares typically hold 2–4 hours" is true and derived from SlickTrip's real-time data. Urgency without fabrication. Trust without pressure.
04
Exactly Two Response Options
Act or defer. "Book now" and "Snooze 1hr." Never more. Every additional option halves the chance of any action being taken.
05
Astra's Voice, Not a System's
"This is your window" — not "price alert triggered." Astra writes every alert as if she's nudging a friend who asked her to keep an eye on something.
05 · Astra AI Feature Suite

What Astra can do
that no one else does.

These eight capabilities either don't exist in the market or exist in fragmented, inferior forms. Each one is buildable on SlickTrip's existing real-time data infrastructure.

Flagship

Astra Watches. You Live Your Life.

The core differentiator: Astra isn't a search engine you return to — she's a background agent you brief once. Tell her about a trip. She monitors flights, seats, hotels, cars, nearby airports, and flexible dates continuously. When something changes in a way that matters to you, she texts. This is the "travel agent in your pocket" made literal — not a metaphor for a better search form, but an actual agent doing the work.

User briefing: "I want to fly to Barcelona in September, 10 nights, budget £700 return, flexible on exact dates"

Astra, 3 days later: "Sep 4–14 just opened at £618. That's £182 under your budget and the only availability under £700 I've seen this month. 6 seats. Want me to hold your eye on it?"
AI Feature

Fare Personality Score

Every route gets an AI-generated profile: how volatile it is, when prices historically bottom out, the ideal booking window, and whether it's safe to wait. Users stop guessing about timing.

JFK → LHR · Economy
Volatility: Moderate · Book sweet spot: 3–5 wks ahead · Spikes: Fri/Sun · Avg saving by waiting correctly: $94
AI Feature

Calendar Flex Finder

With calendar permission, Astra identifies actual free windows and cross-references them with the cheapest travel windows for your saved destinations. She suggests trips you didn't know you had time for.

Astra: "You have 9 days free Oct 9–17. That's Japan's cheapest month by 28%. JFK → NRT is $489 right now — your best opening all year."
AI Feature

Group Sync

Share a trip link. Friends connect their home airports. Astra finds the cheapest combination across all departure cities — solving the hardest group travel coordination problem no competitor touches.

3 friends: NYC · Chicago · LA → Cancún
All fly Aug 12: JFK $371 · ORD $298 · LAX $412
Total: $1,081 vs $1,440 meeting in NYC first
AI Feature

AI Seat Scout

Astra learns seating preferences (aisle, legroom, quiet zones) and alerts when a preferred seat opens on a tracked flight — not just when the price drops. The seat is part of the deal.

Astra: "32A on AA176 just opened — aisle, extra legroom, £0 upgrade (normally £45). Only happens when someone cancels. Want it?"
AI Feature

Disruption Predictor

Before booking, Astra cross-references weather, airport congestion data, and historical delay rates for the specific route and day. She flags risk and suggests alternatives before it costs you.

⚠ Risk: JFK → ORD · Friday Aug 9
34% delay rate · Storm forecast · Thu Aug 8: $22 cheaper, 91% on-time
AI Feature

Trip Mood Engine

"I need to escape" — Astra interprets mood signals and surfaces emotionally relevant destinations with live deals. Travel is driven by feeling. Search should understand that.

User: "I need something different"
Astra: "Bali · quiet, 4.8★ beaches · $612 from JFK — 3rd lowest this year. Or Lisbon: walkable, unhurried, $298."
AI Feature

Post-Booking Companion

Once a trip is booked, Astra shifts from deal-hunter to companion: check-in reminders with seat upgrade checks, gate change alerts, packing suggestions based on destination weather, baggage rules for the specific airline. The pocket agent that doesn't clock off at booking.

T-48hrs · JFK → LHR:
✦ Check-in opens in 2 hours
✦ London: 🌧 rain · pack waterproof
✦ Seat 32A · still yours
✦ Upgrade to Premium: £89 available
